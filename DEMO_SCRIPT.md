# 1.5-Minute NEXTSTORE Demo Script

## 0:00–0:12 — Hook
"Retailers don't have only a location problem. They have an investment decision problem. A site can have high footfall and still destroy value through rent, competition or cannibalisation."

## 0:12–0:27 — What NEXTSTORE does
"NEXTSTORE is an AI-assisted Retail Expansion Decision OS. It compares demand quality, economics, competition, accessibility and network impact, then ranks locations by net incremental value rather than top-line sales alone."

## 0:27–0:45 — Demonstrate the cockpit
Show the dashboard and ranking table.

"Here, Civic Square is the current base-case recommendation at roughly ₹1.26 crore of annual net incremental value and 36% ROI on the illustrative dataset. East Market has the highest footfall, but the model shows why footfall alone is not enough: income quality and competitive pressure matter too."

## 0:45–1:05 — Show Decision Breaker
Click the stress-test view.

"But we don't stop at a recommendation. NEXTSTORE asks: what could make this decision wrong? The Decision Breaker calculates the modeled footfall shock that can make the next-best location overtake the leader."

## 1:05–1:18 — Show scenario interactivity
Move the footfall or rent slider and show ranking movement.

"Management can change the assumptions live. The portfolio economics recalculate instantly, so the user can see the trade-off rather than relying on a static score."

## 1:18–1:30 — Close
"The final AI layer is represented here as a simulated LLM synthesis: it explains the deterministic outputs and points management to the next human validation action. The data is synthetic; production use would calibrate the coefficients with real P&Ls, mobility, competitor and catchment data."
